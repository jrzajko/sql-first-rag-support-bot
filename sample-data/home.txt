Welcome to Lantern Lane Yoga, an independent yoga and Pilates studio tucked down a cobbled lane in East London. We opened in 2019 in a converted lantern workshop, and today we run around 70 classes a week across two studios, a Reformer room and our garden sauna cabin.

Whether you have never stepped on a mat or you have practised for years, there is a place for you here. Our teachers keep classes small, friendly and unhurried.

What we offer
- Yoga: Vinyasa, Hatha, Yin, Restorative and our Back Care class
- Mat Pilates and Reformer Pilates
- Pregnancy and postnatal classes
- Our garden sauna cabin with a cold-water bucket shower
- Foundations courses for complete beginners
- Studio and treatment room hire
- A 200-hour yoga teacher training

New here? Our Intro Offer gives you three weeks of unlimited studio classes for £45. See our Prices page for details.

Community classes: every Sunday at 4pm we run a pay-what-you-can community class, open to everyone. All money raised goes to our partner charity, which runs yoga sessions in local primary schools.
