# 200-Hour Yoga Teacher Training | Lantern Lane Yoga

Our 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training runs once a year over nine months, with one weekend a month at the studio plus online study. It covers asana and alignment, sequencing, anatomy, philosophy, teaching skills and the business of teaching yoga. Graduates can register with Yoga Alliance.

Who can apply
You'll need at least 18 months of regular yoga practice and to be 18 or over. You don't need to want to teach: many trainees join to deepen their own practice.

Cost
The full course costs £2,950. An early-bird price of £2,650 is available until three months before the start date, and payment can be spread over six monthly instalments.

How to apply
Complete the application form on our website and we'll invite you for an informal chat with the course leads. Places are limited to 16 trainees.
