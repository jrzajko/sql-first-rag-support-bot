Concessions
Students and over-65s get 15% off class passes and memberships. Please bring valid ID when you first sign in.

Memberships
Memberships renew automatically each month. You can cancel with 30 days' notice. You can pause your membership once per calendar year, for between 2 and 8 weeks, with at least 5 days' notice before your next payment date.

You can cancel a studio class booking up to 12 hours before it starts and the class will be returned to your account. Reformer bookings need 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations and no-shows lose the class.
