Pregnancy Yoga
Our Pregnancy Yoga classes are designed for every stage of pregnancy from 14 weeks onwards. They focus on gentle movement, breathing for labour and building strength for the months ahead. No previous yoga experience is needed. Please let your teacher know how many weeks pregnant you are when you arrive.

If you already practise yoga regularly, you're welcome to keep attending Level 1 general classes in your first trimester, but please tell your teacher you're pregnant. From 14 weeks we recommend switching to Pregnancy Yoga.

General Pilates and Reformer Pilates classes are not suitable during pregnancy. We run a dedicated Pregnancy Pilates class instead, suitable from 14 weeks.

Postnatal Yoga with baby
Postnatal Yoga is suitable from 8 weeks after birth (10 weeks after a caesarean), once you've had your postnatal check with your GP. Babies are welcome in the class until they're crawling. Bring a blanket or mat for your baby to lie on, and feel free to stop to feed or change them at any time.

Baby Massage
A four-week course where you learn simple massage techniques for your baby. Suitable for babies from 6 weeks to pre-crawling. £55 for the course.

A note on health
Our teachers are trained in pregnancy and postnatal yoga, but classes are not medical advice. If you have any complications, please check with your midwife or GP before joining.
