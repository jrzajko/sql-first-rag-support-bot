Class levels
Every class on our timetable is marked with a level:
- Level 1: for beginners and anyone wanting a slower pace. No experience needed. We focus on alignment, breath and simple options for every pose.
- Level 2: for students with at least three months of regular practice.
- All Levels: open to everyone, but expect a moderate level of fitness, as these classes can be physically demanding.

Not sure where to start? Book a Level 1 class or join our four-week Foundations course, which covers the basic postures, breathing and how to use props safely. Foundations runs once a month and costs £60 for all four sessions.

Yoga
- Vinyasa Flow: dynamic, breath-led sequences. Level 2 and All Levels.
- Hatha: slower, held postures with clear instruction. Level 1 and All Levels.
- Yin: long, passive holds working into connective tissue. All Levels. Yin is not recommended if you have a current spinal disc injury; please speak to your teacher first.
- Restorative: fully supported postures with bolsters and blankets. Level 1.
- Back Care Yoga: a gentle class focusing on mobility and core strength for the back. Level 1. This class is not a substitute for medical treatment; if you have a back injury or ongoing pain, please check with your GP or physiotherapist before joining.

Pilates
- Mat Pilates: core strength, posture and control. Level 1 and Level 2.
- Reformer Pilates: classes are held in our Reformer room with a maximum of 6 people, so every client gets close attention. Your first Reformer class must be a Level 1 class, where the teacher shows you how to set up and use the machine safely. Please wear grip socks for Reformer classes; you can buy them at reception for £8.

What to bring
We provide mats, blocks, bolsters and blankets. Bring a water bottle and wear comfortable clothes you can move in. A small towel is useful for Vinyasa classes.

Timetable
Class times change each term, so please check the live timetable at https://www.example.com/schedule/ before you travel.

Arriving and late arrival
Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit so we can sign you in. Doors close 5 minutes after a class starts and late entry is not possible after that, out of respect for the class.
