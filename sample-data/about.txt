About Lantern Lane Yoga
We started Lantern Lane Yoga in 2019 to create a calm, welcoming studio where people of every age, shape and ability feel at home. Our building was once a workshop that made street lanterns, which is where our name comes from. Today we have two studios, a Reformer room, a treatment room and a garden with our sauna cabin.

Community and charity
Every Sunday at 4pm we run a pay-what-you-can community class, open to everyone. All money raised goes to our partner charity, which brings free yoga sessions to local primary schools. We also offer a number of free class passes each term for people on low incomes; ask at reception in confidence.

Find us
Lantern Lane Yoga, 3 Lantern Lane, London E2. We're the building with the green door, a two-minute walk from the station.

Getting here
The nearest station is a 2-minute walk away and several bus routes stop on the high street. We have secure bike racks in the courtyard. There is no parking at the studio; the nearest pay-and-display parking is on the high street.
